Auto-resolution rules
✓Weight gap ≥ 25: Higher-trust source wins. Lower source stored as historical note. Confidence = winner's weight × 0.9.
✗Weight gap < 25: Never auto-resolve. Escalate to reasoning alerts. Both versions preserved until human decides.
✗Any conflict on critical path: Escalate regardless of weight gap. Critical paths = auth flows, payment flows, data deletion.
~Spec vs. observed behavior: Always treat as a potential bug, not a knowledge conflict. Route to findings queue, not resolution queue.
